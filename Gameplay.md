## Control

- **[Proposed]** Keyboard-first. Typing is the core action, so everything that can be done by keyboard should be.
- Mouse: pick up / move documents on the desk, drag the highlighter, click the phone, stamps.
- **[Open]** Should highlighting/omitting also be keyboard-driven (select passage with arrows + hotkey) for speed-typist players?

## Game Loop

### Day loop **[Proposed]**

1. **Morning** — national (Grand PP) theme (see [[Sound]]), commute screen, newspaper headline from one of the three state outlets, written from documents the player processed yesterday. Countdown to the Centennial.
2. **Briefing** — the Supervisor's new *Directives* and *Lexicon* entries arrive on the desk.
3. **Shift** (timed, clock on the wall) — repeat the document loop below.
4. **Interruptions** — Supervisor's line, coffee lady, fellow new hires (until they are "transferred").
5. **End of day** — performance report (WPM, accuracy, processed count, citations) and salary.
6. **Evening** — spend salary: rent, heat, food, and supporting Katriona and Basil, who spent their savings on the player's degree. Salary is a bank ledger entry, never cash (currency reform). Run out of money → lose condition.

### Document loop (the core 30–90 seconds) **[Proposed]**

1. **Receive** the hand-written source document.
2. **Read & check** it against today's Directives and the Lexicon.
3. **Highlight** passages (discrepancy / sensitive / evidence).
4. **Mark omissions** — struck passages must *not* be typed.
5. **Type** the official version into the terminal, applying Lexicon substitutions on the fly.
6. **File** it: Certify · Return (discrepancy) · Archive · Incinerate.
7. **Instant feedback** (bell / buzzer), mistakes become citations at end of day.

Key design point: **typing is the commit.** In *Papers, Please* the stamp is the decision; here the decision is what the player chooses to type, what they skip, and which words they replace.

## Rank gating **[Proposed]**

The canon ranks ([[Story]] → *Career Progression*) gate the verbs:

| Rank | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Validity Officer** | Read, Highlight discrepancies, Type verbatim, Certify / Return |
| **Correction Officer** | + Omit, + Lexicon substitutions, + Revisions of past documents — only where the Supervisor instructs |
| **Ministry Accolade** | + Free edit with no instruction, + Dictation; can also secretly restore original facts |

**[Open]** Promotion triggers: proposal = days served + performance score threshold + a scripted "loyalty test" document at each rank-up.

## Mechanics

- **Type** — transcribe the document. Scored on WPM and accuracy *against the rules* (typing the original word where the Lexicon demands a substitution counts as an error).
- **Highlight** — mark passages. Correct highlights earn accuracy; the Ministry checks what you highlighted.
- **Omit** — strike passages from the record. Omitted text is skipped while typing.
- **Classify** — assign a category/tier. **[Open]** Less central now that the canon Ministry is about authentication and certification rather than secrecy tiers; keep as a light secondary task or drop.
- **Rules** — daily *Directives* (what to omit/flag) and the *Lexicon* (word substitutions), in a rulebook on the desk, growing over time.
- **Coffee** — **[Proposed]** stamina/focus resource: typing accuracy or speed decays late in the shift; coffee restores it but costs time and money. The coffee lady is also a character (see endings).
- **Phone** — the Supervisor's line (offkey ring, see [[Sound]]) with urgent rule changes mid-shift; family. **[Open]** The bible leaves the Supervisor's communication method open; phone + written notes keeps him faceless and masked.
- **[Proposed] Revisions** — from Correction Officer rank, documents the player certified on earlier days come back to be rewritten. The player's own past work is the evidence of the lie.
- **[Proposed] Pocket / Keep** — the player can secretly keep a copy of evidence instead of incinerating it. Risky (random desk inspections), and it feeds the Escape variants / leak ending.
- **[Proposed] Dictation** — Ministry Accolade: no source document; the Supervisor dictates and the player types a fabrication.
- **[Proposed] Restore** — Ministry Accolade: when given free edit, the player may type the original facts instead. Not flagged by the Ministry immediately; raises the hidden score and the risk of discovery.

## Objectives

- Ministry objective (visible): hit quota, keep accuracy, climb the hierarchy.
- Personal objective (visible): pay for rent/heat/food.
- Conscience objective (hidden, never scored on screen): what happened to the truth. This is the bible's "hidden morality or complicity score".

**[Canon]** The final choice is **Escape**, **Atonement** or **Succession** (see [[Story]]). Conditions and variants are proposed in [[Specification]] → *Endings*.

Earlier win-condition ideas (now variants of the canon endings):

- become head of the Ministry → **Succession** (next Supervisor)
- succeed in an act of espionage → **Escape**, successful variant
- **[Proposed]** successfully leak the kept evidence → candidate 4th ending

## Lose Conditions

- run out of money(?)
- get caught during espionage(?) → **Escape**, failed variant
- **[Proposed]** too many citations → reassignment / "re-education"
- **[Proposed]** kept evidence found during a desk inspection
